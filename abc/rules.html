How It Works

Rules and scoring for Udbhoron '19 Predictor FWC-26

Getting Started

  1. Register with your full name and mobile number.
  2. One account per mobile number — keep your number unique.
  3. Browse upcoming World Cup 2026 matches and submit your predictions.
  4. Climb the leaderboard and compete for rewards at the end of the tournament!

Making Predictions

  • Predict the score for both teams (e.g. Argentina 2 - 1 Brazil).
  • The predicted winner is derived from your score (higher score wins, equal = Draw).
  • One prediction per match — submissions cannot be edited.
  • Predictions close automatically at kickoff time.
  • You cannot predict after a match has started.

Point System

ScenarioPointsExample
Correct winner + exact score 5 points Predict 2-1, Result 2-1
Correct winner, wrong score 3 points Predict 2-1, Result 3-1
Wrong winner 0 points Predict 2-1 Argentina, Result 1-0 Brazil

Leaderboard

  • Points from all matches are summed into your total points.
  • The leaderboard ranks players by total points (highest first).
  • Leaderboard updates in real-time after match results are published.
  • Top-ranked participants at the end of the tournament receive rewards.

Fair Play

  • Play fair — one account per person.
  • Predictions are locked at kickoff on both frontend and backend.
  • Match results are published by the tournament admin.
  • Points are calculated automatically when results are published.
